Where Southern Virginia University students can get help with math, and when. These are the Fall 2026 hours. Help sessions and tutoring are free and open to every student, with no appointment or sign-up.

[Download the printable flyer (PDF)](/files/Math-Resources-Flyer.pdf)

## 1. Professor help sessions (Magnolia 109)

| Professor | Days | Time | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dr. Bell** | Tue & Thu | 8am–12pm | Any student, any math question |
| **Dr. McBride** | Tue & Thu | 12–4pm | Any student, any math question |
| **Prof. Cheney** | Mon, Wed & Fri | 2:30–4:30pm | Intermediate/College Algebra students |

**Just walk in.** Dr. Bell and Dr. McBride take questions from any student in any math course. You don't need an appointment or to be in their class. On **Tuesdays and Thursdays**, one of them is in Magnolia 109 from **8am to 4pm**. The room also has a **small math library** you are welcome to use.

## 2. Math tutoring (Magnolia 100)

| | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Peer tutors** | 4–8pm | 4–8pm | 4–8pm | 4–8pm | |

Drop in any evening Monday through Thursday. Tutoring is free and open to every student, with no sign-up. Bring your homework and whatever you are stuck on.

**Specialist tutors for MAT 106.** Email us and we will find a time that works for you.

Questions? Email the tutors at [math.tutor@svu.edu](mailto:math.tutor@svu.edu).

## 3. Testing Center (Robey 10)

| | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Proctored testing** | | 4–10pm | 4–10pm | 4–10pm | |

Open Tuesday through Thursday evenings for proctored exams. Ask your instructor which of your tests are given here, and arrive with enough time left in the evening to finish.

## 4. Private tutors in the community

Some students want a standing weekly session, help outside the posted hours, or sustained work on one course. The tutors below offer that privately. **Each sets their own rate**, and you arrange times and payment with them directly.

| Tutor | Courses | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Camille Wagenman** ([camille.wagenman@svu.edu](mailto:camille.wagenman@svu.edu)) | All but MAT 106 | Set by tutor |

*Please read:* These tutors are **independent**. They are not necessarily employed, supervised, or screened by Southern Virginia University, and the university neither sets nor collects their fees. All scheduling and payment is arranged directly between you and the tutor. Being listed here is not an endorsement. **Everything in sections 1–3 is free.**

**Are you a tutor?** Currently enrolled students, alumni, and community members are welcome on this list. [Email me](mailto:jared.mcbride@svu.edu) your name, the courses you cover, and how students should reach you. The list is refreshed each semester.

**Not sure where to start?** Try the free help sessions and peer tutoring first. Most students find what they need there.
