An introduction to probability and statistics: describing data, experimental design, regression, probability models, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, and analysis of variance.

## About the course

This is the statistics course most SVU students take. The catalog topics are measures of center and variability, correlation, experimental design, linear regression, probability, normal distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, chi-square, F, and t distributions, and analysis of variance.

Two things shape how I teach it. First, we do our computing in Excel or Google Sheets. Graphing calculators can do a great deal of statistics, but they are not what people use in the workforce, so students are tested on doing the analysis in a spreadsheet. Second, statistics does not live in a textbook. It lives in the news, in advertising, in published research, and in the arguments people make to persuade you of things. "In the Wild" assignments send students out to find statistics there, analyze the claims being made, and fit our probability models to real situations.

Students prepare for each class with a lesson guide and a short pre-reading quiz, so class time can go to discussion and activities. Three midterm "Celebrations of Knowledge," one per unit, and a comprehensive final round out the course.

**Textbook:** [*Introductory Statistics*](https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics) from OpenStax, free online.

**Prerequisite:** MAT 114: Finite Mathematics or MAT 115: College Algebra.

## Helpful resources

- [Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/). A visual, interactive introduction to probability and statistics. Excellent for building intuition about sampling distributions and confidence intervals.
- [StatKey](https://www.lock5stat.com/StatKey/). Free in-browser tools for simulating sampling, bootstrap confidence intervals, and randomization tests.
- [Khan Academy: Statistics and Probability](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability). Extra explanations and practice problems for every unit.
- [My YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@JaredMcBrideMath). Lesson videos I have recorded.
