Vectors, matrices, systems of equations, vector spaces, determinants, linear transformations, eigenvalues, and eigenvectors.

## About the course

Linear algebra is, for many students, the first mathematics course where the concepts matter more than the computations, and where each concept rests on the ones before it. I teach it with that in mind.

Students prepare for class with a pre-reading guide and a short lesson quiz. Because so much depends on a small set of foundational definitions and theorems, students are given a list of "Toughloves," facts to internalize deeply and know from memory. "Math the World" assignments and projects connect the theory to applications, and three midterm "Celebrations of Knowledge" and a final exam measure progress.

**Textbook:** *Linear Algebra and Its Applications*, 5th edition, by David C. Lay, Steven R. Lay, and Judi J. McDonald.

**Prerequisite:** MAT 241 (or concurrent enrollment), or consent of the instructor.
