Number systems and operations for prospective elementary school teachers, together with the pedagogical knowledge needed to teach them to K–6 learners.

## About the course

This is the first of a two-semester sequence for future elementary teachers. It covers set theory; number systems, their structure, basic operations, and properties; number theory; and ratio, proportion, and percent. The aim is not only that students can do this mathematics, but that they understand it deeply enough to explain *why* it works to a child.

Each section of the text gets two kinds of class meeting: a Demo Day and a Deeper Day, with hands-on work using a kit of classroom manipulatives. Students write task reflections, complete lesson guides and homework with full reasoning, and finish the semester by writing a Final Lesson Plan of their own.

**Textbook:** *Mathematics for Elementary Teachers: A Conceptual Approach*, 10th edition, by Bennett, Burton, Nelson, and Ediger. MAT 305 covers Chapters 1–6; MAT 306 covers Chapters 7–12.

**Prerequisite:** MAT 115: College Algebra.
