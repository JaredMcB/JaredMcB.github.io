The trigonometric functions: the unit circle, graphs, identities, equations, and triangle applications, as preparation for calculus.

## About the course

Trigonometry is a companion to College Algebra that uses the trigonometry chapters of the same textbook. It is the bridge between algebra and calculus: students leave able to move comfortably among angles, the unit circle, graphs, and identities.

**Textbook:** [*Algebra and Trigonometry 2e*](https://openstax.org/details/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e) from OpenStax, free online.

**Prerequisite:** MAT 115: College Algebra (may be taken concurrently).

## Helpful resources

- [Desmos graphing calculator](https://www.desmos.com/calculator). Graph sine and cosine with sliders for amplitude, period, and phase shift, and the transformations stop being mysterious.
- [Khan Academy: Trigonometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/trigonometry). Extra explanations and practice, especially good for the unit circle.
- [Paul's Online Math Notes](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/). The algebra and trigonometry review is a good summary of what calculus will expect you to know.
